# Items -- Abilities (Jokers)

**status:** research-integrated <!-- scaffold | research-integrated | live-observed | reconciled -->
**last_reconciled:** 2026-10-20

Jokers are the game's passive-ability cards: 150 expected, 150 located (balatrowiki and the Fandom wiki agree on every name, rarity, cost and effect; the Steam store page also says 150). Rarity split Common 61, Uncommon 64, Rare 20, Legendary 5; 105 are available from the start, 45 are locked (40 by condition, 5 Legendary via a late-game Spectral card). Effects are paraphrased. Page references: `P1-n` and `P2-Rn` resolve in the Sources list at the foot of this file.

## Where Jokers can appear (unlock chain, by rarity)

_source: P1 research 2026-10-19 [P1-1][P1-2][P1-6][P1-8][P1-15][P1-16][P1-22] · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 0 · category: mainline · spoiler: none · vector: item_

- **Common:** shop slot (70% weight); Buffoon Packs; Judgement (random Joker); Riff-Raff (creates 2 Commons); Top-up Tag (2 Commons).
- **Uncommon:** shop (25%); Buffoon Packs; Judgement; Uncommon Tag.
- **Rare:** shop (5%); Buffoon Packs; Judgement; Rare Tag (once any Rare is discovered); one Spectral card.
- **Duplicates:** a Joker you own does not appear again unless a duplicate-allowing Joker is held (see the locked block). Some cards and Jokers can copy any owned Joker including Legendaries (Negative is removed from the copy). Edition Tags make the next shop Joker Foil, Holographic, Polychrome or Negative.
- **How unlocking works:** a locked Joker shows its condition in the Collection, sometimes with a progress counter; whether locked Jokers are excluded from every pool until unlocked is implied by the 105 / 45 split but not stated. [Hypothesis -- unverified]
- **Discovery is separate from unlocking:** an unlocked Joker counts as discovered once bought or obtained in an unseeded run; buying it and quitting to the menu still discovers it.

## The 150 Jokers

The 150 Jokers are split into three blocks by spoiler tier: 105 available from the start (`none`), 40 locked behind an unlock condition (`progression`) and the 5 Legendary Jokers (`late-game`). Row numbers run 1-150 across the three blocks. The unlock conditions for the 40 conditional Jokers were also checked against balatrowiki's Unlocks page and a launch-day Prima Games list (P2-R3, P2-R11); they agree except where noted (one locked Joker is missing from Prima; one locked Joker's price differs in the Chinese wiki).

## Run-scoped unlock windows (can be lost within a run)

Several unlock conditions are decided inside a single run and are lost for that run once broken; every one can be tried again on a later run, so nothing is lost for the profile: Wee Joker (win in 18 or fewer rounds; needs blind skips), Merry Andy (win in 12 or fewer rounds), The Duo, The Trio, The Family, The Order and The Tribe (win without ever playing a Pair, Three of a Kind, Four of a Kind, Straight or Flush respectively; broken the first time you play that hand), and Invisible Joker (win without ever holding more than 4 Jokers).

## Legendary Jokers

All five (Canio, Triboulet, Yorick, Chicot, Perkeo) come only from The Soul (a Spectral card that needs an empty Joker slot; about 0.3% per card in Arcana and Spectral Packs; Séance, Sixth Sense and Ghost Deck shop spawns never produce it). They never carry Eternal, Perishable or Rental outside challenge setups. With Omen Globe, Arcana Packs can hold Spectral cards (20% per card); The Soul's odds in that case were not found `[unlock-chain incomplete -- source/condition unknown]`.

# Items -- Consumables (Tarot, Planet, Spectral)

**status:** research-integrated <!-- scaffold | research-integrated | live-observed | reconciled -->
**last_reconciled:** 2026-10-20

One-use cards: Tarot (22), Planet (12), Spectral (18), plus the booster packs that deliver them. All consumables are available on a fresh profile; each is "discovered" when bought or used in an unseeded run. Page references: `P1-n` resolve in the Sources list at the foot.

## Planet (expected 12 -- the Steam store page says 11; located 12)

Hand and per-level gains are in the table in `mechanics.md` (Scoring). Price $3. Sources: Celestial Packs (Telescope guarantees your most-played hand's Planet in them); the shop; Blue Seal (creates the Planet for the final hand of the round if the card is held); The High Priestess; one late-game Spectral card (+1 level to every hand). The 3 secret Planets (Planet X, Ceres, Eris) appear only after their hand has been played in the current run. The store's "11" against the wikis' 12 (9 regular plus 3 secret) is `[Contradicted across sources -- see notes]`; the store count may predate the third secret Planet or exclude one. ## Booster Packs (5 types x 3 sizes)

Each shop offers 2 packs; the first shop always has a Normal Buffoon Pack. Tags and Booster Packs are separate categories from consumables (Tags are skip rewards, listed in `mechanics.md`).

_source: P1 research 2026-10-19 [P1-16][P1-22] · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 0 · category: mainline · spoiler: none · vector: item_

| Type | Contents | Normal ($4) | Jumbo ($6) | Mega ($8) | Type weight (chance) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Arcana | Tarot (Spectral with one voucher) | choose 1 of 3 | 1 of 5 | 2 of 5 | 6.5 (28.99%) |
| Celestial | Planet | 1 of 3 | 1 of 5 | 2 of 5 | 6.5 (28.99%) |
| Standard | playing cards, often with modifiers | 1 of 3 | 1 of 5 | 2 of 5 | 6.5 (28.99%) |
| Buffoon | Jokers | 1 of 2 | 1 of 4 | 2 of 4 | 1.95 (8.7%) |
| Spectral | Spectral | 1 of 2 | 1 of 4 | 2 of 4 | 0.97 (4.33%) |

_source: P1 research 2026-10-19 [P1-16][P1-22] · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 0 · category: mainline · spoiler: none · vector: item_

- Each size has its own weight (Normal Arcana weighs 4, 17.84%; Mega Spectral 0.07, 0.31%). One voucher gives each Arcana Pack card a 20% chance to be a Spectral. Odds of editions, enhancements and seals in Standard Packs were not found `[location inventory incomplete]`.
- Count mismatch: the wiki lists 15 type-and-size variants; the P2 research says the Collection counts 32 Booster Pack entries. The 32 may count art variants of the same pack, but no source says so. `[Contradicted across sources -- see notes]` [Hypothesis -- unverified for the cause]

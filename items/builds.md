## Mainstream guides converge on four principles

1. Prioritise economy early and reach the $25 interest cap by about Ante 3.
2. Move from chips and flat +Mult to xMult by about Ante 5-6, because flat values fall off.
3. Put independent xMult Jokers on the right (only independent effects care about position; see the scoring exceptions in `mechanics.md`).
4. Use the copy Jokers on your strongest Joker.

## Flush-focused build

Core: Droll Joker and Crafty Joker early; The Tribe (X2) as the xMult payoff; Four Fingers; Smeared Joker; level Flush with Jupiter; The Star, The Moon, The Sun, The World and Sigil narrow the deck to one suit. Checkered Deck makes Flushes and Straight Flushes viable (Diamond and Club Jokers are "fundamentally useless" there). Gap: no read source gave a dedicated flush loadout; the list is assembled from item effects. Risks: the suit-debuff bosses (The Club, The Goad, The Window, The Head) disable a mono-suit deck; with Smeared Joker each suit boss hits half the deck (inferred). The Tribe is unlocked by winning without playing a Flush.

## Pair / of-a-kind build

Payoffs: the X-Mult Pair Jokers (X2, X3, X4 tiers); Jolly and Sly Joker early; Spare Trousers for Two Pair; Card Sharp; level the hand with Mercury or Venus. for two decks (see the deck advice below), one guide says "pairs are the most reliable", favouring Joker scaling over enhancements, and another says to pick an easily drawn hand such as pairs. PhotoChad variant: Photograph plus a retrigger Joker, played as a High Card or Pair led by a face card (ideally a King), gives roughly x8 or more. Trap: the X-Mult Pair Jokers are locked behind winning without playing that hand, so the unlock fights the build it rewards (mechanism observation, not advice from a source). ## Scaling-mult (xMult) build

Held-in-hand engine: Baron plus Mime with a King-heavy deck (thin it with The Hanged Man); about X11 with 5 Kings and Mime (one author's figure). Red Seals and Steel Kings with a Legendary Joker or a late-game Spectral card is the standard Endless engine; it scales on the same order as Endless requirements only by adding copy-Joker copies each Ante. Self-scalers: Hologram, Constellation, Vampire (a copy Joker copies its xMult but not the eating), Obelisk, Campfire, Lucky Cat and a few locked Jokers; Madness with all-Eternal Jokers scales with no losses on Black Stake and above.

## Hybrid / utility build

Early economy Jokers (Cloud 9, To the Moon, Golden Joker, Rocket, Faceless Joker, Mail-In Rebate, To Do List, Bull), then the copy Jokers onto the best scorer; one guide adds a few round-and-slot Jokers (Drunkard, Burglar) for flexibility. On the balancing deck chips matter as much as Mult because of the balancing; two Reddit commenters still prefer xMult there.

## Where guides disagree

- Flat +Mult Jokers: one guide says skip non-scaling hand-specific Jokers after about Ante 4 and calls Lusty and Wrathful weak late; another recommends +Mult scalers (Green Joker, Supernova, Half Joker, Popcorn, Red Card, Fibonacci and a few locked Jokers) for the top difficulty. They partly agree: both prefer scaling sources.
- Skip Tags: see `mechanics.md`.
- Chips value: a Reddit commenter says chip Jokers are outclassed by Planets around Ante 5 (Runner and one locked Joker are exceptions); a top-difficulty guide still lists a chips package (Runner, Square Joker, Castle, Banner and a few locked Jokers).

## Deck- and stake-specific advice (Gold Stake, one guide)

Black Deck is hardest (about 30 attempts; restarted for a Round 1 Investment Tag); Plasma easiest. Green Deck: beat rounds in one hand, avoid hand- or discard-scaling Jokers (Green Joker, Castle). Abandoned: Straights and Four of a Kind, avoid face-card Jokers. Painted: hand-based builds. Anaglyph: hold skip tags until about Ante 5-6; avoid Negative Tags because of Perishable and Rental. Erratic: check the card distribution or reset. Ghost: use Hex on a keeper Joker; Eternal Jokers pair well with it. Rental Jokers: avoid early; later they cost nothing if bought and sold within the same round, which also feeds Campfire (good Rental targets: Hallucination, Chaos the Clown, Cartomancer, Mail-In Rebate). Perishable Jokers are fine when fewer than 5 rounds from beating Ante 8. Erratic Deck rerolls can start the 30-suit-card Joker unlocks; a Painted Deck run helps Invisible Joker's unlock (Korean wiki tips).

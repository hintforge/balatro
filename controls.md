## PC (mouse and keyboard)

- Left-click: select or deselect a card or pack, choose a blind, press a button.
- Hold left-click and drag: reorder a card. Joker order matters for scoring (see `mechanics.md`).
- Right-click: deselect all selected cards.
- Hold R: restart the run with the same deck and stake, reusing a manually set seed.
- Esc: open Options when no other menu is open.

## Controllers

- PlayStation: Cross select, Circle back, L1/R1 for buy, use or sell. Xbox: A, B, LB/RB. Switch: B select, A back (swapped from Xbox), L/R.
- The game is playable with one stick and four buttons. Play and discard are on-screen buttons, not dedicated inputs.

## Accessibility

A Family Gaming Database page (22 features): fully playable with the mouse alone; no simultaneous or rapid button presses; long holds optional (Hold-R restart is the only hold); screen shake can be disabled; busy backgrounds can be stilled; colour-blind friendly through double coding; separate music and sound-effect volume; playable without hearing. No remapping is documented by it or by the wiki Controls page. Role split: this file owns input and access; the option values for Screenshake, High Contrast Cards, Reduced Motion and the volume sliders live in `settings.md`.
